Our Story

Bred with Pride in the Heart of Punjab

Jay Stud Farm was founded in 2021 by Mr. Sidharth Gupta in Amritsar, and named after his son. What began as one man's passion has grown into one of North India's most respected homes for the Marwari breed.

Recognised for their grace, courage and historical significance, the Marwari is more than a horse to us. It is a living heritage. Every animal here is raised with individual care, expert nutrition and the discipline of the show ring, so that each one carries the breed forward with dignity.

  • Over 80 Marwari horses across stallions, mares, fillies and colts
  • Multiple first-place wins at Punjab, Empire and Hanumangarh horse shows
  • A dedicated team of 50+ grooms, trainers and veterinary staff

The Breed

Why the Marwari Is Unlike Any Other Horse

Born in the deserts of Marwar, Rajasthan, and ridden into battle by Rajput cavalry for centuries, the Marwari is India's own royal horse.

01

The Signature Ears

Inward-curving ears that touch at the tips. No other breed in the world carries this mark, and it can turn a full 180 degrees.

02

A Warrior's Heritage

Bred by the Rathores of Marwar from the 12th century, these horses carried kings and were trusted never to leave a fallen rider.

03

Desert Endurance

Hardy, sure-footed and able to thrive on little water and in extreme heat, with a natural ambling gait called the revaal.

04

Loyal and Intelligent

Known for a deep bond with their handlers, Marwaris are quick to learn, proud in bearing and gentle at home.
