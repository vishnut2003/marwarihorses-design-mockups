About Us

Jay Stud Farm is devoted to breeding Marwari horses of grace, strength and character.

Founded in Amritsar in 2021 by Sidharth Gupta and named after his son, the farm has grown into one of North India's most respected homes for the breed. More than eighty horses are cared for by a team of over fifty grooms, trainers and veterinary staff.

  1. 01
    Proven bloodlines

    Stallions and broodmares selected from the Varkhana line and other respected Marwari families.

  2. 02
    Individual care

    Expert nutrition, daily handling and veterinary oversight for every horse on the farm.

  3. 03
    Show-ring discipline

    Prepared and presented to the standard that has earned first places at Empire, Punjab and Hanumangarh.

Sidharth Gupta
Founder, Jay Stud Farm
